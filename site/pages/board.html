How it works.

  1. It reads the brief. What is being built, for whom, the register, and the shape. On its own it asks for whatever is missing in one message.

  2. It finds specific pages, never galleries. Four or five in your category at the ceiling, one or two from outside the web or outside the category, and one deliberately against the grain, labelled. Each one is opened and checked. It never invents a URL.

  3. One line per page, written as a principle. "The work starts a third of the way down" is a principle. "Clean, modern design" is not.

  4. You keep two and name one you would not want. The one you would not want is usually the most specific thing you say all session.

  5. Your picks become principles, never a copy. They go into the brief as behaviour. The URLs stay out, because a brief that names a site produces a derivative of it.
