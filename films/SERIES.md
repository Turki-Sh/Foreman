# Four useful Foreman films

These films extend Before the code with four separate jobs. Each runs 40 seconds. They use original illustrative interfaces, not recordings or benchmark claims. The demonstration persona is Turki Alshuaibi. No external brand footage, paid media, narrator or new generated imagery is used.

## Research and editorial decisions

- Apple, AirPods Pro 3 product film: https://www.apple.com/airpods-pro/. Inspected during the first film production. Adopt isolation, scale and one benefit at a time. A software playbook needs demonstrations rather than hardware glamour shots.
- OpenAI, Introducing canvas: https://openai.com/index/introducing-canvas/. Played the 48-second writing demonstration and inspected its opening notes-to-prompt view and later revised-document view. Keep the object being changed in view so an edit has a visible consequence. The page's coding explanation also supports targeted revisions rather than wholesale replacement.
- Anthropic, Building verification loops in Claude Code: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mQZB0l-rhxE, linked from https://claude.com/resources/videos. Inspected the official player, opening explanation and middle sequence. Its useful structure is a named workflow with a check, not a generic capability list. Presenter footage is not appropriate without Turki's recorded contribution; these films remain music-led.
- Google Workspace: https://workspace.google.com/solutions/ai/. Reviewed the official use-case organization and product examples. Each demonstration starts with a job in context. No claim that the entire embedded campaign video was watched.
- Google Flow: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/google-flow-veo-ai-filmmaking-tool/. Retain the input-to-result causal sequence researched for Before the code.
- Remotion's official skills and production guidance remain the renderer reference: https://github.com/remotion-dev/skills and https://www.remotion.dev/docs/ai/skills. Do not install unrelated video generators or replace the existing production pipeline.

These observations guide editing; they are not evidence of conversion lift or an assertion that a visual style guarantees reach.

## Four jobs

1. Find your direction: a vague request becomes a specific audience, a visual comparison and usable decisions. Editorial reference studies lead into a brief and a page composition. The studies are authored examples, not screenshots of uncredited websites.
2. Fix what feels wrong: identify vague copy, competing actions and mobile overflow; show corrections and a mobile recheck. Dark inspection stage with the same page retained through the diagnosis.
3. Colour that works: move from a material to colour roles, verify a pair, demonstrate light/dark roles and export tokens. The neutral palette comes from ink and paper.
4. Before you share: inspect the address, improve a share card, recover from a missing URL, check discovery files and submit. The reserved example address is illustrative. Indexing remains pending because a submission is not an indexing guarantee.

## Evidence

The palette contrast examples use sRGB relative luminance: #aaaaaa on #ffffff is 2.3231230535:1, displayed as 2.32:1; #17191b on #ffffff is 17.6276485838:1, displayed as 17.63:1. Normal text AA threshold is 4.5:1. Source: https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html. Classification uses unrounded values.

## Delivery

Four editable Remotion compositions, deterministic locally synthesized scores, 1080p H.264 High level 4.1, AAC, fast-start MP4. Each film has 33 VTT and SRT caption editions, a watch URL, visible transcript, creator metadata, video sitemap entry and a distinct social composition for every locale. Native controls, no autoplay. Existing film and personal website remain intact.

Rebuild: run `python films/production/series_score.py`, then `python films/render_series.py` from the repository root. `scripts/build_series_og.cjs` builds the social images from extracted final film frames. The site build owns the generated HTML and captions.
