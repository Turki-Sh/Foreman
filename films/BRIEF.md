# Foreman film: Before the code

## Brief

One 60-second English product film for Foreman's /films/ watch page and social sharing. Captions in all 33 site languages. Author and demonstration name: Turki Alshuaibi. No changes to the personal portfolio or Sarjy.

The story follows a prompt becoming a brief, then a build. This is an authored product explanation, not a recording of a real agent session. Do not attribute the example to a particular model or present illustrative interfaces as Foreman's own software UI. Foreman is a markdown playbook used by a coding agent.

## Visual system

Black ink, white paper, fine drafting lines and the current hard-hat mark. Large, lightly weighted type; close views of one decision at a time. A fine drawing line establishes the opening frame. References expand into a board, swatches align with a type specimen, and the resulting brief becomes a window onto the real Foreman website. No unrelated stock footage, fake metrics or synthetic testimonials.

## Sequence

- 0-6: Before the code. A drawing line establishes the frame.
- 6-13: Turki's request. Two decisions replace a vague prompt.
- 13-22: References. Composition, typography and hierarchy become choices.
- 22-30: System. Type, colour and spacing settle into a coherent page.
- 30-38: Brief. The decisions converge on BRIEF.md. Approval precedes the build.
- 38-48: Build. Eight steps and the real Foreman website.
- 48-54: Toolbelt. Four distinct tools, each with a visual purpose.
- 54-60: Foreman by Turki Alshuaibi. Free, open source, direct URL.
