# Foreman films: new review cut

Private review cut. Not a production release or a claim of creative approval.

All five stories, scripts and compositions were rewritten. The original and revision 3 previews remain available. Product version stays 3.0.1.

## Films

- An idea worth building: a default-filled starting point becomes an audience decision, reference study, brief, build and review.
- Find your direction: saved pages become deliberate choices about hierarchy, spacing and composition.
- Something feels wrong: inspect a page, replace a vague headline, simplify its actions, improve contrast and check mobile.
- Colour with a job to do: material studies become colour roles, contrast checks, two themes and reusable tokens.
- Before the first click: inspect the domain, share preview, missing-page recovery, metadata and owner-dependent work.

The terminal scenes and example pages are authored demonstrations of Foreman's workflow. They are not recordings of a fresh agent run, benchmark results or a separate Foreman application. Board's illustrated studies are not presented as a list of actual researched websites. The full Foreman homepage shown in the film is a local capture of the current site.

## Production

- Remotion with deterministic 60 fps scene clocks; 1920 by 1080 review exports.
- Three.js material ribbons, paper sheets, colour chips, frames and orbiting surfaces. Physical materials and a studio environment, with lighting reduced after the first proof was overexposed.
- Existing approved architecture and robot assets reused. No new generated images, external film footage, paid service or cloned voice.
- Free local Kokoro stock English voice, am_michael. Scripts omit repeated personal-name narration.
- No music, drum loop or bass impact. The review soundtrack adds sparse, quiet, unpitched interface clicks to the narration.
- Captions in all 33 site languages, retimed to the rewritten narration. Translations reviewed by the three previously authorized language agents.
- Contrast demonstration uses #546079 and #f5f2e9 on #101828. Calculated ratios are 2.8109:1 and 15.8536:1, displayed as 2.8:1 and 15.9:1.

Creative acceptance remains Turki's. Passing a render, type check or media audit is not evidence that the films meet his taste.
