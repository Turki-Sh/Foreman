Research

Wider randomization
wasn’t more robust.

It was making the policy timid.

The quadruped pipeline started in simulation. Retraining after each hardware change took most of a day, and the policy kept overfitting to the simulator.

Adding system identification and narrowing the randomization ranges brought retraining to about 40 percent of the previous time.
