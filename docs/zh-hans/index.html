先规划网站，再开始编程。

提出建站需求，Foreman 就会发送开场卡片并问一个问题。想少回答问题，可以说 survey ，它会先阅读你的代码仓库、现有网站和简历。想深入讨论，可以说 stress test ，它会对每个回答再追问一次。三种模式需要确定的事项相同。

比较模式 ↗

默认模式。完整的需求访谈，每次只问一个问题。

help me build my portfolio

Foreman sends its card. MODE reads Blueprint.

A portfolio. I have my CV and screenshots from a robotics project, nothing else yet.

FOREMAN · 2/8 · DECISIONS Logged: you have a CV and robotics screenshots. Still missing: any written copy, a domain, a logo, and a hero image or media. Who is the one reader you actually care about? Name a role, such as a hiring manager at a robotics lab or a research group lead. "Everyone" and "recruiters" in general don't count.

Research group leads hiring PhD students in legged robotics.

FOREMAN · 2/8 · DECISIONS Reader: a research group lead hiring PhD students in legged robotics. That reader scans fast and has a stack of CVs, so the page has to make you the exception inside ninety seconds. After those ninety seconds, what is the one thing you want them to do? Email you, open a paper, watch a demo, download the CV. Pick one.

尽量减少提问。先调研，再整理成一页决策清单，每项都附上来源。

I need a landing page for my app Linkwell, no time for a long process, can you just get it moving

Foreman sends its card. MODE reads Survey, because the message said there is no time.

It's Linkwell, a local-first notes app. Everything is in the README in this folder. I have a logo (logo.svg, not here yet), no domain.

It reads the README before it asks anything, then:

FOREMAN · SURVEY · 2/8 · DECISIONS
Reader
To confirm
Action
Download Linkwell
Evidence
310 missed links in 2,400 notes
Scope
No signup, pricing, analytics, or blog
The download link in the README is github.com/example/linkwell/releases. I'll treat that as a placeholder until you give me the real URL. Who is the one reader: Obsidian users with a big vault, people who keep notes in plain Markdown, or someone else?

最深入的模式。进行相同的需求访谈，但每个回答在确认前都会再追问一次。

/foreman:foreman stress test

Foreman sends its card. MODE reads Stress test.

A portfolio. I have my CV and screenshots from a robotics project, nothing else yet.

FOREMAN · STRESS TEST · 2/8 · DECISIONS ✓ LOCKED · 1/8 ORIENT · subject: portfolio · have: CV, robotics project screenshots · missing: written copy, other projects, logo, domain Who is the one reader you actually care about? Give me a named role, such as "research group lead hiring a PhD student" or "robotics startup CTO". "Recruiters" and "everyone" don't count.

Research group leads hiring PhD students in legged robotics.

FOREMAN · STRESS TEST · 2/8 · DECISIONS That reader is specific enough to build for. One push before it locks: a group lead skims dozens of applicants a week and has seen every "passionate about robotics" page. What would make that lead close your page within ten seconds?

起点

明确网站用途，收集现有资料。

Site: research portfolio
Reader: robotics research group lead
Material: CV and project notes

决策

确定读者、预期行动、内容和范围。

Action: email about a PhD position
Include: research, background, contact
Leave out: blog, analytics, contact form

Typography: type scale and reading widths

四种工具。当你需要的时候使用它们。

可以单独调用，也可以在会话中途使用而不丢失进度。在 Claude Code 中，每个工具都有斜杠命令。其他智能体则直接用自然语言提出请求。

Board →

/foreman:board

为您的简报选择的真实网站，作为列表或带有屏幕截图的视觉板，以值得借鉴的原则结尾。

A reference for navigation.
A reference for typography.
A reason to use each one.

Punch list →

/foreman:punch-list https://your-site.com

检查任何网站，包括并非用 Foreman 制作的网站。找出套路化或未完成的设计问题，按优先级排列，并为每项提供修复建议。

13 items: 2 broken, 4 unfinished, 4 generated, 3 notes
1. Header nav at 375. Nothing replaces it, so phone
   visitors cannot reach Install or switch theme.

Palette →

/foreman:palette

从你指定的材质生成三套配色，检查对比度，并用实际内容展示。你来选择其中一套。

A  Cyanotype print    #1B4A73  #F2BE3C
B  Drafting film      #F3F4F1  #1F4F7A
C  Graphite, redline  #1F2327  #EC6A55

Launch →

/foreman:launch

域名、DNS、SSL、共享卡、真正的 404、站点地图和索引，一次一步，适合在任何地方构建的站点。

done   Email untouched   MX and SPF still on the apex
done   404 status        /nope returns 404, own page
open   Search Console    submit sitemap.xml
