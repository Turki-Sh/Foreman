# Code of conduct

Foreman is maintained by one person, and its issues and pull requests are a workplace. Treat them like one.

## Expected

- Be specific. Describe what you saw, what you expected, and what happened, the same standard the playbook holds an agent to.
- Disagree with the work, never with the person. Saying a rule produced a bad build is the most useful thing you can do here.
- Assume the other person is capable, and that a short answer is a busy one, not a hostile one.
- Credit people. If someone's report or idea shaped yours, say so.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or in private messages.
- Discriminatory language or behaviour of any kind, including about someone's language, country, or level of English.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption: repeated off-topic posts, brigading, or reopening a decision that was closed without new evidence.
- Spam, including promotion of unrelated products in issues and pull requests.

## Enforcement

Turki Alshuaibi maintains the repository and enforces this code. Reports go to turki@turkialshuaibi.com, or privately through the **Security** tab if the report involves a security issue. Reports are read in confidence.

Depending on what happened, the response ranges from a private note, to removal of the content, to a temporary or permanent ban from the repository. Whoever is reported gets the chance to respond before a ban, except where the behaviour puts someone at risk.

This code applies in this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone represents the project.
