It answers with questions, not code.

Ask for a site and Foreman sends its card and one question. Say survey for the fewest questions: it reads your repo, your site and your CV instead of asking. Say stress test for the most: every answer gets pushed once. Every decision gets made in all three.

How the three modes work

The default. The full interview, one question at a time.

help me build my portfolio

Foreman sends its card. MODE reads Blueprint.

A portfolio. I have my CV and screenshots from a robotics project, nothing else yet.

FOREMAN · 2/8 · DECISIONS Logged: you have a CV and robotics screenshots. Still missing: any written copy, a domain, a logo, and a hero image or media. Who is the one reader you actually care about? Name a role, such as a hiring manager at a robotics lab or a research group lead. "Everyone" and "recruiters" in general don't count.

Research group leads hiring PhD students in legged robotics.

FOREMAN · 2/8 · DECISIONS Reader: a research group lead hiring PhD students in legged robotics. That reader scans fast and has a stack of CVs, so the page has to make you the exception inside ninety seconds. After those ninety seconds, what is the one thing you want them to do? Email you, open a paper, watch a demo, download the CV. Pick one.

The fewest questions. Research first, then one sheet with a source on every line.

Four tools that work without the interview.

Call one on its own, or in the middle of a session without losing your place. In Claude Code each is a slash command. Anywhere else, ask in plain words.

Board →

/foreman:board

Real sites chosen for your brief, as a list or a visual board with screenshots, ending in the principles worth taking from them.

oxide.computer: figures are numbered and treated
as plates. Take this: label the diagrams of how
Foreman works as numbered figures.

Punch list →

/foreman:punch-list https://your-site.com

Audits any site, including ones Foreman never touched, for what makes it look AI-generated or unfinished, ranked, with a fix for each.

13 items: 2 broken, 4 unfinished, 4 generated, 3 notes
1. Header nav at 375. Nothing replaces it, so phone
   visitors cannot reach Install or switch theme.

Palette →

/foreman:palette

Three palettes from a material you name, contrast checked, on your real content. You pick one.

Launch →

/foreman:launch

Domain, DNS, SSL, the share card, a real 404, the sitemap and indexing, one step at a time, for a site built anywhere.

done   Email untouched   MX and SPF still on the apex
done   404 status        /nope returns 404, own page
open   Search Console    submit sitemap.xml (yours)

Markdown and templates. Nothing executes.

No scripts, no hooks, no MCP servers, no network calls. You can read every line before you trust it, which is the only sane way to install something that talks to your agent.
